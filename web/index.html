What's going on here

The model

Decoder-only transformer: BPE tokenizer (1,024 tokens, trained from scratch on Persian), 4 layers, 8 heads, width 256, context of 128 tokens. Trained for ~30 min on 4 CPU cores. Weights are shipped as float16 (7 MB).

The traps

Following Meeus et al. (ICML 2024), fake couplets were inserted 1–16 times, verbatim or with 20% of the words changed each time (“fuzzy”, as in mosaic memory). Matched fakes that were never inserted serve as controls.

Honest caveats

Membership inference is statistical: a single score can be wrong, and the thresholds come from one training run. Exact AUCs, confidence intervals and what didn't work are in RESULTS.md.
